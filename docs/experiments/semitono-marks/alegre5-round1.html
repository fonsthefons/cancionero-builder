Click a syllable = the next note of that line starts there (the reciting note starts on the first syllable already). Click a note button first to place or move a specific note. Right-click a syllable = remove the notes on it. Chords follow their note; to move a chord, click its red button, then the syllable. Click between two syllables to join them (‿ = sung as one, sinalefa) or split them. Change the bar (1 / 2 / flexa) with the menu. Everything is saved in this browser as you go.
